## Question 1
### ==Part (a)==
Convert the decimal number 187 into its binary and hexadecimal equivalents. Show your working.

**Binary Conversion Steps** Divide the number repeatedly by 2 and read the remainders from bottom to top to get the binary value.

- 187 ÷ 2 = 93, remainder **1**   *this is the LSB*
- 93 ÷ 2 = 46, remainder **1**
- 46 ÷ 2 = 23, remainder **0**
- 23 ÷ 2 = 11, remainder **1**
- 11 ÷ 2 = 5, remainder **1**
- 5 ÷ 2 = 2, remainder **1**
- 2 ÷ 2 = 1, remainder **0**
- 1 ÷ 2 = 0, remainder **1**      *this is the MSB*
